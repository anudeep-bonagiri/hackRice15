# HackRice 2024 - Requirements & Information

## Important Dates & Deadlines

- **Devpost Submission Deadline**: Sunday, 9/21, 9:00 AM

## Submission Requirements

### Devpost Submission
- Must submit a 3-4 minute video to Devpost before the deadline
- When providing "Additional info" about your project, you MUST CHOOSE AT MOST 1 TRACK
- You CAN choose MULTIPLE CHALLENGES

### Video Submission Outline (3-4 minutes total)

1. **Intro** - 30 seconds
   - Project name
   - Team members
   - Track/challenge selected
   - Project purpose
   - Technologies used

2. **Demo** - 2 minutes
   - Show your project in action!

3. **Technical Design and Implementation** - 30 seconds
   - High-level overview of how you designed your code

4. **Impact** - 30 seconds
   - Real-world value of your project
   - How you'd improve it in the future
